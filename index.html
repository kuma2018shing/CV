About me

I'm a self-taught software developer based in Cape Town. I started with a software engineering bootcamp at HyperionDev, and I've been building ever since — moving from small web apps into data analysis, backtesting and automated tooling.

Most of my recent work sits at the intersection of code and markets: pulling data from exchange APIs, testing whether an idea actually holds up statistically, and being willing to throw it out when it doesn't. I care more about measuring honestly than shipping something that looks clever.

Outside of code, I hold my own in a hot wings challenge. Ghost peppers and Carolina Reapers are a normal snack. 🌶️
